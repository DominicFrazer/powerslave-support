# I'm not an AI Guru. I'm an LLM user.

**Dominic Frazer-Imregh**
*Published on LinkedIn, 28 May 2026*

---

Like a racing driver, I've become expert at using the tool. I have opinions about driving it well, I write about what I've figured out from actually shipping things with it. That's the whole pitch.

But I leave the engineering to someone else. Don't call me a mechanic when all I do is sit behind the wheel and press on the gas. I can't predict what the next model will do. I don't sell courses. If you want guru content, there's plenty of it.

And, if you're professing to be an AI specialist and crooning about "what I learnt today", maybe downgrade your title or think more carefully before you pick up a microphone.
