Privacy Policy

The short version

TVApp does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising. The app connects only to the media server that you configure.

What data the app handles

Server connection details. When you connect TVApp to your Jellyfin server, the app stores your server address and login credentials on your device only so you don't have to sign in every time. This information is never sent to PowerSlave Developments.

Your media and playback activity. Browsing your library, streaming, and playback progress are exchanged directly between your device and your own Jellyfin server. We have no access to your server or its contents.

Data we collect

None. PowerSlave Developments operates no servers that receive data from the app, and the app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party advertising or analytics services. Your data is not shared with anyone. The only network connections the app makes are to the Jellyfin server you provide.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
