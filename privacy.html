Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Map Wanderer policy

The short version

None of our apps collect your personal information. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no user account. PowerSlave Developments runs no server that receives your data. What you create stays on your device.

Which apps this policy covers

  • TVApp — a client for your own Jellyfin media server
  • Contagion — a roguelike raid game
  • JetPack — a 2D action shooter
  • TreeWise — a tree identification guide and sightings log

Map Wanderer is not covered here. It uses third-party mapping and routing services, so it has its own policy — see the Map Wanderer privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. PowerSlave Developments operates no back-end server that receives information from these apps. They contain no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking identifiers. We do not build profiles, and we have nothing to sell or share, because we never receive it in the first place.

We are not able to identify you, contact you, or see how you use any of these apps unless you choose to email us.

What each app stores on your device

Content you create or configure is held locally, under your control. It is not transmitted to PowerSlave Developments.

TVApp. Your Jellyfin server address and sign-in credentials, so you do not have to enter them each time, plus your app settings. Browsing, streaming and playback progress are exchanged directly between your device and your own Jellyfin server — playback position is saved back to that server so you can resume on another device. We have no access to your server or anything on it.

TreeWise. Sightings you log, including any photos you attach, and your app settings.

Contagion and JetPack. Game progress, scores and settings.

Removing an app from your device deletes the data it stored. Because none of it reaches us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request you need to make.

Network connections

TVApp connects to the Jellyfin server address you enter, and to nothing else of ours. That server is yours; how it handles your data is governed by your own setup, not by us.

The other apps do not send your personal information anywhere. Where an app needs to reach the internet for a functional reason, the request carries only what is needed to serve it, and no copy comes to us.

Apple operates the App Store and may collect its own data around downloads, purchases and crash reports under Apple's privacy policy. That relationship is between you and Apple; any crash reports we can see through Apple are aggregated and do not identify you.

Children's privacy

These apps do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because they do not collect information from anyone at all.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — to access it, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for those rights to apply to. If you believe otherwise, email us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
