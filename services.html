Who we work with

We keep the practice small, so we're selective — but the range is broad.

  • Founders & startups — who need a credible product shipped fast, and often a fractional CTO to make the early architecture, commercial and hiring calls.
  • Scale-ups & enterprise — adding AI to existing products, modernising ageing mobile codebases, or bringing in senior firepower for a defined, high-stakes build.
  • Regulated & security-critical clients — in finance, payments and communications, where PCI-DSS, secure messaging and audited delivery matter.
  • Hardware & connected-device teams — building IoT or robotics products that need equally strong mobile and AI front-ends.
