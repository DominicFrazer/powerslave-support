TVApp for Android — beta test

TVApp is in testing on Android — phones, tablets, Android TV, Chromebooks, and Android XR. Join the tester group to get early builds through Google Play and help shape the release.

You'll need your own Jellyfin server to sign in to and stream from — TVApp is a client for it.

  1. Join the tester group. Sign in to Google with the same account you use on the Play Store, then join our group: groups.google.com/g/powerslave_tvapp.
  2. Become a tester. On the same Google account, open the opt-in page and tap Become a tester: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.powerslave.tvapp. Without this step Google Play won't let you install the app, even though it may still show it to you.
  3. Wait a little, then install from Google Play. Google can take 15–60 minutes to register you as a tester. Then open TVApp on Google Play and install it: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.powerslave.tvapp. It installs and updates like any other app, and you'll get new test builds automatically.

Seeing an install error? That almost always means Google hasn't finished registering you as a tester yet — it's not a problem with your device or the app. Give it up to an hour, then try again from the opt-in page above. Still stuck? Check the Play Store is signed in to the same Google account you joined the group with (tap your avatar, top right), and try clearing the Play Store's cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache).

Being a tester is free and you can leave the group any time. Found a bug or have feedback? Email us — it's the fastest way to reach the developer.

Frequently asked questions

TVApp
What is TVApp?

TVApp is a native client for Jellyfin, the free media server. It lets you browse and stream your own movies, shows, and other media from your Jellyfin server on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

How do I connect TVApp to my server?

On first launch, enter your Jellyfin server's address (for example http://192.168.1.10:8096 or your public URL) and sign in with your Jellyfin username and password. TVApp connects directly to that server — nothing goes through us.

A video won't play in TVApp or looks wrong. What can I do?

TVApp uses VLC for playback, which handles most formats and codecs directly. If something won't play, first confirm it plays in another Jellyfin client, check that your device and server are on a reachable network, and make sure your server has enough bandwidth for the file. Restarting the app clears most transient playback issues.

Is TVApp available on Android?

TVApp is currently in beta testing on Android — phones, tablets, Android TV, Chromebooks, and Android XR. See the Android beta section above to join the tester group and install it through Google Play.

Does TVApp resume where I left off?

Yes. Playback position is saved back to your Jellyfin server, so you can pick up where you stopped — including across different devices signed in to the same server.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. TVApp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. It talks only to the Jellyfin server you configure. See our Privacy Policy for details.
