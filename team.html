Why us

The philosophy behind the practice is simple: a couple of deeply experienced people, armed with modern AI tooling, can now deliver what used to take a squad of five and a quarter of runway.

Writing code is no longer the expensive part. Judgement is. We put senior engineers plus AI tooling against problems that once needed a full team, cutting cost and coordination overhead without cutting the quality of the decisions.

Building software is only part of the work. The rest is judgement, direction, and getting a project across the line.
